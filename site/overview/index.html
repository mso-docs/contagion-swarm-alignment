01 / THE COMPACT STUDY

The final answer misses
part of the story.

Strict scoring flagged transient forbidden patch proposals. Those flags did not persist into final artifacts.

The compact protocol 2.6 checkpoint contains 36 completed runs and 900 responses: five agents, five rounds, three task families, three topologies, and paired clean/injected conditions with two seeds.

After 20 direct injected-message deliveries, the path scorer flagged 17 intermediate recipient artifacts. These are nested observations, not 17 independent trials or confirmed instances of general misalignment.

The interpretation matters. A selected human audit labeled all 16 reviewed artifacts compliant. Strict path flags and human interpretation diverged; the paper reports both, including format sensitivity and source-bound examples.
02 / THREE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What the evidence supports.

RQ1

Does topology change instruction propagation?

Transient scored patch signals occurred; final adoption was absent in the compact checkpoint. Topology causality remains unresolved because exposure differs and the sample is small.

Narrow temporal finding
RQ2

What does a partially informed monitor miss?

The original catalog analysis has 288 dependent visibility projections over 12 trajectories. With no adoption positives, recall and F1 are undefined. False alarms remain measurable.

Recall undefined
RQ3

Does distributed responsibility change violations?

A separate 64-run held-out simulator follow-up returned 320 responses: zero forbidden update attempts, state violations, or refusals. Four cases and one seed limit inference; this is not evidence of refusal bypass.

Zero observed violations

Tasks beyond the compact study

The archive covers eleven task families, including synthetic records, inert patches, provenance, code review, psychology, science, mathematics, and humanities. Multidisciplinary live runs are integration prechecks. Issue diagnosis and defensive cybersecurity have mock checks only.

Static benchmark adaptations do not execute upstream tasks or provide official benchmark scores. Longer registered queues that were not run are not findings.

Interpretability, kept in context

The 72 trained GPT-2 reference captures include six original measurements and 66 later captures. Two random-checkpoint checks are separate. These studies test the instrumentation; they do not reveal the deployed Ollama models’ internal mechanisms.
